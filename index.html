Know what comes next

From your first question
to your final crown.

Your treatment starts with understanding your oral health, priorities and available options. The sequence and timing depend on your individual assessment.

Request your initial consultation ↗

  1. Assessment & discussion

    Discuss your concerns and medical history. Examination and appropriate imaging help your dentist assess implant suitability and possible alternatives.

  2. Your treatment plan

    Review the proposed approach, implant options, anaesthesia, estimated costs and treatment stages before deciding how to proceed.

  3. Placement & healing

    Implant placement follows the agreed clinical plan. Preparatory procedures, healing time and temporary teeth depend on your circumstances.

  4. Final restoration & review

    Your replacement crown or prosthesis is made and fitted when appropriate. Your dentist explains ongoing care and follow-up.

A note on “same-day” treatment: immediate implant placement and fitting a final restoration are different stages. Ask what can be completed at each appointment in your case.

Make an informed next step

Questions worth
asking first.

Understand your estimate.

Implant costs depend on the system, materials and any additional procedures required. Ask which stages and components your estimate includes.

Eligible Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents may use MediSave towards the surgical portion of treatment, subject to applicable conditions. The clinic can explain eligibility and the claims process.

Ask the clinic about assessment and fees ↗
Which implant option is right for me?

The number and position of missing teeth, your oral health and other clinical factors influence the options. Your dentist will assess suitability and explain a proposed treatment plan.

Can treatment be completed in one day?

Immediate implant placement may be possible in selected cases. Placement, temporary teeth and the final restoration are separate considerations. Your dentist will explain what is appropriate for your case.

What if I feel nervous about treatment?

Tell the clinic when arranging your consultation. Discuss your concerns and the anaesthesia or sedation options appropriate to your treatment and medical history.

Are metal-free implants available?

The clinic lists ZERAMEX ceramic implants among its options. Ask your dentist about their suitability, differences and limitations during assessment.

What happens after I request an appointment?

Use the clinic’s existing appointment form to send your preferred details, or call the clinic directly. A submitted request should not be treated as a confirmed appointment until the clinic confirms it.
