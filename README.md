# The Dental Implant Centre — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

The Dental Implant Centre is a dental practice in Raffles Place, Singapore, focused on implant treatment. Its website serves adults considering replacement of missing or failing teeth, including Singapore residents and expatriates, and supports consultation-led treatment enquiries.

The redesigned homepage prioritises treatment choices, clearer patient guidance and straightforward access to a consultation.

## Three Main Fixes

### 1. Earlier Access to Treatment Choices

Treatment pathways now appear immediately after the hero. Previously, visitors passed through the introduction, treatment process and doctors before reaching the main implant options. The revised order helps patients find relevant treatment information sooner.

### 2. Clearer Treatment Explanations

The treatment journey clarifies that anaesthesia, timing and temporary restorations depend on individual assessment. It distinguishes immediate implant placement from the final restoration, addressing conflicting anaesthesia descriptions and uncertainty around “same-day” treatment.

### 3. Simpler Contact Routes

Prominent telephone links sit alongside appointment-request links. Patients can enquire directly without first navigating the existing form’s nine main fields and 52 selectable service choices or deciding which clinical service they need.

## Design Improvements

- **Layout and hierarchy:** Treatment pathways follow the hero, organising the homepage around patients’ initial questions.
- **Content consistency:** The treatment journey provides clearer, consistent explanations of anaesthesia, timing and restorations.
- **User experience:** Prominent contact links make the next step easier to identify alongside treatment information.
